How to read this

Our pick is the team the model thinks is more likely to win. Confidence compares that team with the other side only (a 75% confidence means three chances in four that the picked team beats the other, if the match is not drawn). Chance pick wins is the plain probability of a win, and chance of draw is shown separately: about one in five matches the model is confident about still ends level, so a draw is the usual way a confident pick fails.

Important: forecasts, not advice

These are statistical forecasts from a research model, shown for interest only. They are not betting tips, financial advice or guarantees. Football is uncertain: even picks with the highest confidence lose some of the time, and past accuracy does not guarantee future accuracy. The model does not know about injuries, suspensions, line-ups or managers, and it is behind the bookmakers' own prices in accuracy, so it should not be used to place bets. Where the numbers come from

Forecasts come from our own model, built from past results and match statistics. Historical accuracy is measured on past matches the model had not seen when it made its forecast (seasons , Covid seasons left out). Match results and statistics used to build and test the model are published by football-data.co.uk; fixture dates are from fixturedownload.com and betexplorer.com. This page shows only our own forecasts: no bookmaker odds and no raw data are republished here.
