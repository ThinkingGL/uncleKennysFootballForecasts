How it works
  • Confidence = chance the picked team wins if the match is not drawn. Each day is ranked by it.
  • Win / draw are plain probabilities. A draw is how confident picks usually fail.
  • Past accuracy is measured on matches the model had not seen (, Covid seasons left out).
  • Built from results and shots only: no injuries, line-ups or managers. Less accurate than bookmakers.
  • Accumulator: win chances multiplied, matches treated as independent. Fair odds = 1 ÷ chance. Odds you type are not stored.
  • Risk per pick: Low 70%+, Medium 55-69%, High under 55% (one step worse for new teams or early season). Whole bet: Moderate 50%+, High 25-49%, Very high 10-24%, Extreme under 10%.
  • Updated about weekly; results to . Data: football-data.co.uk, fixturedownload.com, betexplorer.com. No bookmaker odds or raw data are shown.
